## Before store submission
1. Verify that the published support address receives and is monitored for messages.
2. Publish a support page linked from the app's More screen and store listings; include account access, sync conflicts, photos, recovery and data deletion guidance.
3. Review and accurately describe processors (Supabase, GitHub Pages, code/CDN and asset providers), purposes, retention, international transfers, users' rights and jurisdiction-dependent disclosures.
4. Verify Google Play account-deletion policy and Apple account deletion requirements at submission time. In-app deletion exists, but locked-out users need a documented support path.
5. Provide truthful store Data safety / App Privacy disclosures and confirm permissions against the built native binaries.
6. Avoid publishing user emails, credentials, service keys or other personal data in GitHub.

No change to user accounts or the live app is implied by this checklist.
