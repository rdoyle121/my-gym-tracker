# Public support and privacy — launch checklist

Status: **blocked pending app-operator confirmation**. Do not publish personal contact details without approval.

## Required from the app operator
- Public support email address dedicated to My Gym Tracker (must be monitored).
- Public privacy-contact email; this may be the same as the support email if approved.
- Legal name of the operator and applicable postal contact requirements, if any.
- Country/jurisdiction and an appropriate privacy/legal review.
- Expected response process for locked-out users requesting account deletion or privacy assistance.

## Pages currently published (pre-release)
- Privacy information: https://rdoyle121.github.io/my-gym-tracker/cloud-privacy.html
- Account deletion instructions: https://rdoyle121.github.io/my-gym-tracker/cloud-delete-account.html

## Before store submission
1. Add a real monitored support contact to the privacy and account-deletion pages, and verify mail delivery.
2. Publish a support page linked from the app's More screen and store listings; include account access, sync conflicts, photos, recovery and data deletion guidance.
3. Review and accurately describe processors (Supabase, GitHub Pages, code/CDN and asset providers), purposes, retention, international transfers, users' rights and jurisdiction-dependent disclosures.
4. Verify Google Play account-deletion policy and Apple account deletion requirements at submission time. In-app deletion exists, but locked-out users need a documented support path.
5. Provide truthful store Data safety / App Privacy disclosures and confirm permissions against the built native binaries.
6. Avoid publishing user emails, credentials, service keys or other personal data in GitHub.

No change to user accounts or the live app is implied by this checklist.
