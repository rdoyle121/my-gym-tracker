Delete your My Gym Tracker account

This page explains how to request deletion in the app. You cannot delete an account from this information page.

How to delete

  1. Open My Gym Tracker Cloud and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open More → Account & Security.
  3. Select Delete My Account.
  4. Enter the account email and the required deletion confirmation, then accept the final warning.
  5. Wait for the success confirmation and return to the login screen. If an error appears or the response is unclear, do not retry until the account status and function logs have been checked.
Permanent action. Deleting the account removes its Supabase sign-in identity and associated cloud workout, nutrition, measurement, gym-visit, settings and progress-photo records. It cannot be undone.

What is not automatically deleted

Previously exported JSON backups, progress photos held only on your devices, and other device-local copies are not deleted by the server-side account deletion process. Remove these separately on devices you control if desired.
