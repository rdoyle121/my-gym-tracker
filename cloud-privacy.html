Privacy information

My Gym Tracker Cloud — beta
Updated 10 October 2026

Pre-release draft. This page describes current app behaviour observed in its code. It needs a verified operator identity, privacy contact and legal review before public App Store or Google Play release.

Data you provide

When you register, Supabase Authentication handles your email address and sign-in credentials. If you use the features, the app stores workout plans and logs, body weights and measurements, nutrition targets and food entries, shopping lists, workout notes, gym visits and progress photos.

Where it is stored

Workout, nutrition and account-specific settings are stored in your browser and synchronised to Supabase when online and sync succeeds. Progress photos can be kept in browser storage or uploaded manually to a private Supabase Storage bucket. Browser-stored photos are not included in the JSON backup. Uploaded photos may be viewed from another device while signed into the same account.

Location and notifications

The optional nearby-gym search requests your location only after you choose the feature; gym-location search uses OpenStreetMap's Overpass service. Rest-timer alerts may use browser notifications if you grant permission. Device/browser permissions can be changed through system settings.

Sharing and processors

Supabase provides authentication, database and private photo storage. GitHub Pages serves the application website. Some app assets and scripts may be requested from external providers, including jsDelivr and image hosts. No advertising or sale of personal data is implemented in the current app code; this statement is not a complete assessment of third-party processing.

Your choices

You can export workout and nutrition data to JSON from More. You can remove locally saved data and delete individual cloud photos. Under Account & Security, you can request permanent deletion of the signed-in account and associated cloud records. Local exports, locally saved photos and copies on other devices are not automatically deleted by server-side account deletion.

Security and limitations

Account data is protected using Supabase authentication and database access rules; cloud photos are stored in a private bucket with account-owner policies. No online system is risk-free. Cloud sync can pause if the app detects conflicting changes from different devices.
