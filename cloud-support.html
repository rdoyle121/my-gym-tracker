My Gym Tracker support

Questions about your account, workouts, food diary, cloud syncing or progress photos? Contact the app's support team.

Support and privacy contact

Please describe the problem and device type. Never email your password, authentication codes or private progress photos.

Account access and password recovery

On the sign-in screen select Forgot password to request a recovery email. If you cannot access your account, email support.

Cloud syncing

Ensure you are signed into the same account on each device and check the cloud-sync status before switching devices. If a conflict is reported, do not overwrite or clear data until you have reviewed it.

Progress photos

Photos saved only to a device gallery are local. To access a photo on another device, it must be uploaded to your private cloud photo storage. JSON exports do not include photos.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can go to More → Account & Security → Delete My Account. For details or help when locked out, see account-deletion instructions or contact support. Do not repeat a deletion attempt if the outcome is uncertain.

Privacy

Review the privacy information page. App-store legal and data-use disclosures are still being finalised before public release.
